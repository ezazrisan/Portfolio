About

Hi, I'm Ezaz — a graphic designer who also builds the websites the designs live on.

I work across two lanes that most people keep separate: visual identity and WordPress front-end. That means a brand doesn't just get a nice logo — it gets a homepage, a poster series, a product ad and a tote bag that all sound like the same voice.

My process stays simple: understand the audience first, design something that earns a second look, then build it so it actually works on a phone at 3am. No jargon, no bloated builds — just clean, considered work delivered on time.
